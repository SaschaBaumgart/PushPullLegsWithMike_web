2. Principle & Data Processing

You can use our app “Push Pull Legs with Mike” without providing any personal data.

  • No registration / no login: No user account or login is required.
  • No cloud storage: All workouts, exercises, settings and training data you create are stored exclusively on your device in a local database.
  • No internet connection / offline mode: The app works entirely offline and never connects to external servers to transmit data.
  • No tracking & no advertising: No analytics tools, tracking pixels, advertising networks or third-party libraries for data collection are used.

3. Data Deletion

Since all data (workouts, settings) is stored exclusively on your smartphone, it remains there until you delete it manually or uninstall the app from your device. Uninstalling the app irrevocably deletes all locally stored data.

4. Distribution via the App Store

When you download the app via the Google Play Store, the platform operator (Google Ireland Limited) may process data (such as your Google account, IP address and time of download). We have no influence on this data collection; Google’s privacy policy applies.

5. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Because we, as developers, neither collect personal data nor store it on a server, we are technically unable to provide information about personal data, as no such data exists on our side. You are generally entitled to the statutory rights under the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure). With regard to your local app data, you can exercise these rights yourself directly by deleting the app or clearing the app cache in your device settings.
